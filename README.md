# 📊 Blinkit Sales Analysis using Python

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Blinkit's retail sales dataset using Python to uncover meaningful business insights. Through data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), and visualization techniques, the project aims to understand sales performance, customer preferences, and outlet characteristics that influence revenue generation.

The analysis helps identify trends, patterns, and opportunities that can support data-driven business decisions.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

To conduct a comprehensive analysis of Blinkit's sales data and answer key business questions related to:

* Sales Performance
* Product Categories
* Outlet Characteristics
* Customer Preferences
* Revenue Distribution

---

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### Total Sales

Overall revenue generated from all products sold.

### Average Sales

Average revenue generated per product.

### Number of Items

Total count of items available in the dataset.

### Average Rating

Average customer satisfaction score.

---

## 📊 Key Insights

* Regular fat products contributed a significant portion of total sales.
* Certain product categories consistently generated higher revenue.
* Medium-sized outlets showed strong sales performance.
* Outlet location influenced overall revenue generation.
* Older outlets demonstrated stable sales trends compared to newer establishments.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

* Increase focus on high-performing product categories.
* Optimize inventory distribution across outlet locations.
* Improve marketing strategies for underperforming product segments.
* Expand successful outlet formats in high-demand areas.
* Leverage customer preference insights for future business planning.
